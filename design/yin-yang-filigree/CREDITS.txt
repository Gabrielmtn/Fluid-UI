7. Yin yang artwork: Muhammad Bilal, via Vecteezy
-------------------------------------------------
The yin yang at the centre of the knob filigree is adapted from a vector
by Muhammad Bilal, published on Vecteezy (https://www.vecteezy.com, item
80872383). Only the left line-art figure is used. Its outer rim, middle
line, dots and two small rings were removed, and it was recoloured.


Before shipping, confirm the license:
- Free license: commercial use is allowed, but Vecteezy asks to be credited

  in both forms above.
- Pro license: no attribution is required (crediting the artist is still a
