2. **The A2 leak class from round 1 is still breeding.** Remote look-mirror applies persist into the watcher's saved settings via several newly-found paths: the host's light-shift path (`setPath`→`saveSettings`, [14-light-shift.js:539](js/14-light-shift.js:539)), splat In/Out distances (dispatched `input` → persisting handlers, [12-save-load.js:1131](js/12-save-load.js:1131)), `replayLiveColors` force-set ([12-save-load.js:1109](js/12-save-load.js:1109)), and the audio keys aren't in `MP_PERF_LOCAL_KEYS` at all — a painter with `audioMode='full'` **force-opens the watcher's studio drawer and can re-fire their mic prompt**.
3. **Clicking any slider's label jumps its value.** The row-drag forwarder's exclusion list omits `label`/`.value-display` ([06-slider-updater.js:174](js/06-slider-updater.js:174)) — a click on the words maps to that X position and persists. Found independently by two agents; it's the probable origin of "replay speed initializing wrong" and a silent chaos source all test night.
4. **The mirror sheds oversized keys silently.** Any look snapshot over 14,000 chars silently deletes `lightShiftPath` (and more) on both sender and receiver ([06-multiplayer.js:392](js/06-multiplayer.js:392)) — that's the "blown out and white" watcher. Today that's `kaleidoToggle` clobbering the multiplier (A6); it will be something else next time a side-effectful checkbox lands.

## Wave A — verified surgical fixes

### A1. Audio feeding into the brush — S ⭐ CONFIRMED (top priority)
- **Root cause (three couplings, all default-ON):** (1) `Energy → Brush Size` rewrites `config.SPLAT_RADIUS` every ~66Hz tick — ×1.0–2.2 swing, 45ms attack / 380ms release ([22-audio-reactive.js:745](js/22-audio-reactive.js:745)); the brush engine reads it live for dab spacing at emit and again at drain → frame-to-frame width lumps ("jagged"). (2) `Treble → Color Cycle` calls `stepPaletteOnce` as often as every 60ms, overwriting live `pointer.color` **and the color picker** mid-stroke ([22-audio-reactive.js:781](js/22-audio-reactive.js:781), [05n-hotkeys-init.js:224](js/05n-hotkeys-init.js:224)). (3) Bass/mid/treble auto-splats fire velocity kicks (forces 120–1000+) at center/ring/random positions that plow through the in-progress stroke ("stutter", "disconnected"). Jank hypothesis ruled out — the analysis tick is cheap. Verification correction: the spacing-gap mechanism was refuted (spacing overlap absorbs the swing); "disconnected" is the field kicks + color steps.
- **Fix (one commit, 3 files):** flip `mapTrebleToColor`/`mapOverallToSize` defaults to `false` (engine [22-audio-reactive.js:79](js/22-audio-reactive.js:79) AND UI defs [20-mixer-layout.js:3415](js/20-mixer-layout.js:3415) — two sources of truth); delete the Tunnel scene's forced `setMapping('overallToSize', true)` ([30-audio-scenes.js:311](js/30-audio-scenes.js:311)); recommended hardening: gate the SPLAT_RADIUS write + `stepPaletteOnce` on `!BrushEngine.isActive()` so even opted-in routing never touches a live stroke.
- **Known residue:** old snapshots/composer segments carry explicit `true` and re-enable on load — migration is decision 1c.

### A2. Settings lock: light-shift path + "blown out and white" — S ⭐ CONFIRMED
- **Root cause (two real causes):** (1) A freehand light-shift path is ~117 B/point of unrounded doubles ([14-light-shift.js:372](js/14-light-shift.js:372)); anything over ~85 points pushes the snapshot past 14,000 chars and the SHED loop **silently deletes `lightShiftPath`** ([06-multiplayer.js:392](js/06-multiplayer.js:392)). Watcher gets `enableLightShift=true` but an empty path → effect gated off ([05j-update-loop.js:968](js/05j-update-loop.js:968)) → their overexposed whites stay white while the host's are remapped to path hues. Exactly the observed asymmetry. Even short paths reset the watcher's playhead to 0 on every mirror re-apply. (2) The physics-feel divergence is **design**: `physicsResolution`/`visualResolution`/`fpsCap` are deliberately local (`MP_PERF_LOCAL_KEYS`), the QualityGovernor parks weak machines at half res, and the 16ms dt clamp slows sub-60fps clients — surfaced as decision 9, not silently changed.
- **Fix (one commit):** compact the path in `captureLookSnapshot` only (≤64 points, 1-decimal rounding, ~4.1KB — presets keep full fidelity); JSON-equality skip in the apply so mirror re-applies stop resetting the playhead; `if (!window.__mpApplyingRemote) saveSettings()` in `setPath`; `console.warn` in the SHED loop.

### A3. Peers' multi-brush sometimes invisible — XS ⚪ CONFIRMED
- **Root cause:** arm count rides the wire as a per-message `mult`, and two of five `flushDabs` call sites hardcode `1`: the 96-dab overflow flush ([06-multiplayer.js:1530](js/06-multiplayer.js:1530)) and `broadcastPointerUp` ([06-multiplayer.js:1585](js/06-multiplayer.js:1585)). Dense/fast strokes starve the correct-mult timed flush → whole strokes collapse to one arm; every stroke's final chunk collapses regardless → arm-count flicker within one stroke. Both date to 5d5c1ce; 304c48e's audit ran at multiplier 1 and couldn't see it.
- **Fix:** two lines — pass the lexical `animationMultiplier` (typeof-guarded; **not** `window.animationMultiplier`, which 04e leaves stale) at both sites. No wire-format change. Add a mult>1 case to the two-client harness.

### A4. Glow ruining Light Shift — XS ⚪ CONFIRMED
- **Root cause:** glow's halo is added into `color.rgb` *before* the Light Shift recolor and keeps the dye's near-white hue ([05a-shader-core.js:452-474](js/05a-shader-core.js:452)); LS then mixes from that glow-brightened base and the clamp saturates fired pixels back to white — the recolor is arithmetically erased — while the halo spills original-white over the recolored region. With Gate off (the default) the glow sum is unbounded, making this the default-config behavior.
- **Fix (single-file GLSL):** tint the halo through Light Shift before summing — luminance-preserving `glowC = mix(glowC, lightShiftColor*glum, lsIntensity)` inside the existing glow block, gated on `lightShiftEnabled` (uniforms already declared). Bit-identical when LS is off. Verification note: at default intensity 0.5 the fired core lands pale-but-shifted; raising the tint weight is the one-line follow-up if the feel-test wants more.

### A5. Border dye explosion + stylus dies — S PARTLY (latch proven; explosion needs live numbers)

Windows press-and-hold right-click emulation reaches the same branch without touching the barrel.

### B2. Time slider + density scale perceptual remap — M CONFIRMED
- **Root cause (density):** the fader is linear in decay-base `d` but the sim applies `pow(d, dt·60)` — half-life is *hyperbolic* in d (0.85→71ms … 0.993→1.6s, 0.999→11.5s). τ≥0.5s occupies the top ~18% of travel; **all 8 built-in presets sit in the top ~7%**. Exactly "everything happens between 0.9 and 1.0."
- **Root cause (time):** `timeScale` is a pure dt multiplier and dissipation is correctly sim-time-batched — low time doesn't over-fade; what's per-wall-frame is **bilinear resampling blur**, which accumulates ~an order of magnitude more over the same journey at 0.1× (velocity advection has no MacCormack corrector at all). The repo already documented this disease for 144Hz ("visibly dissolved fine dye structure", [05b-shader-sim.js:422](js/05b-shader-sim.js:422)) but only fixed the settle criterion.
- **Fix, Phase A (one commit):** hidden-canonical + visible-proxy fader pattern (ids/wire/stored values byte-identical — the persistence format *is* the DOM element value, so no new id): density proxy maps log-spaced half-life 0.15s→60s, hold-detent at exactly 1.0, growth zone above; time proxy log-mapped with a 1× detent. Reflect from the hidden slider via event + ~250ms poll (Ctrl+scroll and the COS oscillator write valuelessly). Verification adds: the proxy handler needs its own `__mpSettingsLocked` early-return.
- **Fix, Phase B (separate, default-off console tunable — DEBAND ship pattern):** `SIM_STEP_MIN` sim-step batching — below the threshold, accumulate dt debt and run physics passes less often at full step size → slow time becomes slow-but-crisp. Feel-test before defaulting on (decision 10).

### B3. Stroke easing (Splat In/Out) — M ⚪ (DECIDED: brush-side, 3 levers)
Gabriel: *"This is just for the stroke controls, nothing to do with audio... a click doesn't make the entire full brush size pop in if they don't want it."* The audio-envelope reading is **out of scope**; this is the Brush panel's Splat In/Splat Out "Easing".
- **Why it's fast and intense at every setting (all verified):** (a) **the default mode is `instant`** — so a click genuinely is a full-size, full-strength stamp; (b) even in `linear`/`easing`, the ramp scales **radius only**, and the splat shader's center deposit is `exp(0)=1` **regardless of radius** ([05b-shader-sim.js:122](js/05b-shader-sim.js:122)) — so every mode lands full-saturation color in one frame. A radius-only ramp *cannot* fix the flash by construction; (c) the ramp is **distance-based only** — `splatDownTime` is stamped on press and **never read anywhere**, so a stationary click is pinned at the start value forever and can never ease; (d) visible size goes as `sqrt(radius)`, so the ramp is front-loaded — half the travel already buys ~¾ of the look; (e) the Ramp cap of 0.5 canvas-widths is crossed in ~200ms by a fast stroke.
- **Fix (3 levers, decided):** (1) **ramp intensity as well as size** — fold `inMult` into `flowMul` at the three call sites that already funnel through `applyPaintFlow` ([05d-input-replay.js:451](js/05d-input-replay.js:451), :793, [05j-update-loop.js:220](js/05j-update-loop.js:220)), plus the tail at 05j:304; (2) **add a time-based ramp mode** using the already-stamped-but-dead `splatDownTime`, hooked inside `getSplatInMult` — needs a stationary re-emitter so a held click actually re-evaluates; ships as an added mode so the documented speed-independence of `linear`/`easing` is untouched; (3) **widen the Ramp range + finer steps** ([20-mixer-layout.js:1975](js/20-mixer-layout.js:1975)) — free, since these aren't registry params and snapshots apply them unclamped.
- **Default stays `instant`** — out-of-box feel unchanged. Start-floor/curve-shape controls were considered and **cut** (extra UI rows for marginal gain).
- **Gate-mode caveat:** under Color Gate the intensity ramp is local-only — `__splatFlow` isn't captured in stroke events, recordings, or the dab wire, so peers/replays see the un-eased stroke. Additive mode rides everything for free. Pre-existing asymmetry; note in release notes.
- **Adjacent defects found in the same code** (fix in passing, they're one-liners): `pushStrokeEvent` runs *before* `__lastPaintRadius` is set, so every stroke's press event records the **previous** stroke's radius ([05d-input-replay.js:452](js/05d-input-replay.js:452)); the touchstart branch omits `startStroke`/`pushStrokeEvent` entirely, so touch presses open no replay stroke; the release tail bakes flow into color even under Gate ([05j-update-loop.js:304](js/05j-update-loop.js:304)).

### B4. Collider feel + control cleanup — M PARTLY (two verify-mandated additions folded in)
- **Root cause (veil):** after every collider stroke, `_applyColliderPreview` swaps the layer div to an **opaque** alpha-255 grayscale (black where no wall) on a full-canvas 55%-opacity div z-ordered above the sim — a 55% black veil over the artwork with the wall as a grey sticker ([23-depth-collision.js:1051](js/23-depth-collision.js:1051)). Same opaque-at-0.55 applies on preset load and delete-undo (verify addition — fix all entry points via one shared helper).
- **Root cause (paint-on-top):** the splat shader's `osr=min(s³,0.997)` deposition curve leaks **27.1% of every dab's dye into fully-covered walls** at the 0.9 default ([05b-shader-sim.js:56](js/05b-shader-sim.js:56)); under Gate, repeated dabs converge wall texels to the *full* picked color — you can paint the wall over with patience. This is the known-deferred s³ regression.
- **Root cause (muddy controls):** the collision row's Mode select (Block/Slow/Deflect) is wired to **nothing**; Threshold/Invert/🔄 do nothing to physics for painted colliders; stale 💡 hint references controls deleted in 8e3cce9; 🗑️ emoji delete.
- **Fix (one commit):** dye branch gets coverage-only block (velocity keeps the graded curve — advection through leaky walls untouched); on-canvas preview becomes red-tinted alpha-coverage (transparent outside walls) at ~0.3, while-painting film 0.45→~0.25; painted-collider rows reduce to 👁️ ⤢ 🧱 Edit, Strength, text **Delete**; remove the dead Mode select everywhere; keep Threshold/Invert/🔄 only for depth-import colliders. **Verify-mandated: keep the ✂️ toggle (it's the only physics kill-switch — relabel, don't delete)**; preset fields stay for back-compat.
