# Swirl Together: feature trailer (Krita 4 format)

A feature tour built the way the Krita 4.0 release video is built: one
feature group at a time, shown on floating cards over a living backdrop,
named by big two-tier captions. The teaser is a separate cut
(`TRAILER-SCRIPT.md`).

How the Krita video works, measured frame by frame:
`notes/research/krita-4-trailer-format.md`, with stills in
`notes/research/krita-4-video/`.

**Length:** about 3:30. A 4 s title, a 2:56 feature reel (12 groups, 52
captions), then 30 s of call to action and gallery. Krita's reel is about
the same length (2:51) but has only 33 captions, so this one is busier:
about 3.4 s per caption against Krita's 5. If a group feels rushed, cut a
sub-line rather than shortening any caption below 2.5 s.

---

## The format in six rules

1. **Two-tier captions.** Each group opens with a **headline** that slides
   in from the right edge (about 1 s, easing to a stop). Then **sub-lines**
   crossfade into the same spot, one at a time. The headline is about twice
   the size of a sub-line.
2. **Big type.** Sub-lines are about 105 px tall per line at 1080p. White,
   a condensed sans, a soft dark glow behind it (no hard outline), bottom
   right. Each stays up at least 2.5 s. Every caption
   must still read with the video shrunk to 600 px wide (the store
   player).
3. **Cards, not full screen.** Each shot is a cropped card with a soft
   shadow, placed off-centre. Often there are two: the canvas big and low,
   and the control that made it small and high, overlapping. Crop panels
   tight and **scale them up**. Krita's panels are unreadable at store size,
   and that's the one thing to fix about its format.
4. **The backdrop is the sim.** Behind the cards is a slow, blurred,
   dimmed Swirl Together canvas (Krita uses a stock watercolour texture).
   Change its look every few groups so the video travels, the way Krita's
   backdrop drifts from pale blue to purple.
5. **Process, then payoff.** Each group ends on about 1 s of its best
   result alone on the backdrop, with no panel.
6. **Breaths.** Between some groups the cards fade away for about 1 s of
   backdrop only. There are five, marked in the cut.

Kept from Krita on purpose: the title card first, the big-caption grammar,
and the gallery at the end.
Changed on purpose: the cut follows the music (Krita lays music under the
picture and ignores it), there's no dip to white, panels are scaled up, and
the gallery is 30 s instead of 2 minutes.

## The captions are your app's own words

Every caption below is a label from the app as it is today, so the words
are already yours. Rename any of them. Lines that aren't app labels are
plain facts, and each is checked against the code in the table at the end.

Avoid brand names in captions: the Format menu's "TikTok" and "YouTube"
become ratios (9:16, 16:9).

### The gallery (group 13)
Before launch, use **your own canvases**: end every filming sitting by
pressing Save picture on the best frame, and the gallery fills itself.
After launch, a version with players' work and their names, as Krita does
it, makes a good update video. Only use someone's work with their
permission.

## Filming: seven short sittings

Every sitting ends with clips in `Videos\Swirl Together trailer\clips\`
named `<group>-<what>-<take>.mp4` (e.g. `06-wall-02.mp4`), plus **one Save
picture for the gallery**. Record long takes and use the best 2–4 s.

The rig is the same as the teaser's (`TRAILER-SCRIPT.md`, "the rig"):
OBS at 1920×1080, 60 fps, MKV then remux, cursor capture off, the full
desktop app (not the demo or playtest builds). One difference: **card shots
need the UI visible**, so film those with Focus mode **off** and crop the
canvas and panel out of the same recording in the edit (Krita's cards
are crops of one screen recording too). Paint-only shots and the backdrop
use Focus mode and the 16:9 format.

**Ten-minute first step:** film three minutes of backdrop. Pick Slow
marbling or Drifting mist, pull Time low, paint a few slow strokes and then
leave it alone. In the editor, blur it, dim it, and put one card on top
with one caption. If that frame looks right, every other shot is just
filling in the same template.

### The edit (sitting 6)
1. **Build the caption template once**: headline slide-in plus sub-line
   crossfade, at the sizes in rule 2. Every group reuses it.
2. **Lay the music first** and put the group changes and breaths on its
   phrases. The times above bend to the music. If you use two tracks, change
   tracks on a breath (0:44 or 1:30), never inside a group.
3. Cards in, payoffs held, breaths left empty.
4. Check at 600 px wide: every caption readable, every panel card legible.
5. No more than three flashes a second. The kaleidoscope and fast cuts
   are where it could slip.
6. Export 1920×1080, 60 fps, H.264, AAC stereo, ~20 Mbps, twice (one per
   end card). Mix to about −14 LUFS.

I can check the export for you: caption size at store scale, flash
rate, loudness and cut timing, the same way I measured Krita's.

### Upload (sitting 7)
Steam plays the first two trailers before any screenshot. Put whichever
of the two cuts you'd rather a stranger sees first in slot one, and the
other second.
