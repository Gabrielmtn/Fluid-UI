## Rules learned the hard way

- Never register or write `--i`: the app writes an inherited `--i` on every sidebar section (js/20 `stampGroupRows`). The theme's brightness is `--ci`.
- Inline `style=""` beats every layer. Theme paint over script-styled elements needs `!important` (selects, text fields, light panels already do).
- Button colour belongs only in `31-theme-components.css`, or the button audit (js/38, and two suites) fails.
- The material select keeps the system font: `js/29` sizes it by measuring its text.
- No layout changes after boot. Late width snapping re-flowed the bar and resized the canvas under the user, and broke the pen-window suite. Widths are fixed in CSS.
- The screenshot gate captures with reduced motion emulated, transitions off and `--disable-gpu-compositing`. Under reduced motion js/71 settles at once.
- Tests that hard-code coordinates or canvas size break when the bar grows. Fix the test by measuring right before use; never loosen the check.
