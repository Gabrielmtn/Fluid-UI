## How this was measured

- App: main checkout at `a0477e1`, read only, headless Chrome through `scripts/test/radial-menu/driver.js`, reduced motion emulated, `--disable-gpu-compositing`, 1920x1080 and 1440x900 at device scale 1, captured at clip scale 2. Probe scripts were scratch files (never in the repo); the outputs are `prep/work/gaps/g4-dom-outline.txt` (live DOM outline), `g4-measure.json` (computed styles and boxes), `g4-states.txt` (markup of hidden states).
- **Units.** The sidebars carry `zoom: var(--ui-scale)` (`js/20-mixer-layout.js:159-183`): `--ui-scale` is 1.12 at 1920 and 1.00 at 1440. All "now" sizes below are CSS px (computed, before zoom) unless marked "rendered". All targets are the study's CSS px (the study is at zoom 1). Write the targets as CSS px; the zoom scales them like everything else.
- Study: `design/study-lite.html` line numbers ("lite NNN"). The study's Input drawer is the app's Stroke and replay; the study's Simulation drawer is a gravity scope (the app's gravity pad lives in Effects). The study has no sidebar head, so the head's targets are the matching study components.
- `prep/compare/sweep-1920/*-pair.png` and the first round of my own shots were taken with `captureBeyondViewport: true`, which resizes the viewport and re-lays out the sidebars without their scrollbars (content ~14 px wider, values pushed to the edge, the right front cut off). Use the shots listed at the end, which clip inside the viewport.
