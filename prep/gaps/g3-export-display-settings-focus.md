## How this was measured

- **The study has no markup for these four drawers.** Each one is a placeholder: drawer front, bead row and one `.hint` line saying "keeps its current controls and behaviour; each one takes the matching component from the other drawers" (study-lite 1068-1107). Every target below is therefore the matching study component, with its study-lite lines and literal values.
- **Shots** (headless, `scripts/test/radial-menu/driver.js`, `--disable-gpu-compositing`, reduced motion emulated, scale-2 slices taken while scrolling the sidebar's own scroller) are in `prep/work/gaps/shots/`:
  - `focus|display|export|settings-{1920,1440}-x2-N.png` (zoomed slices) and `-full.png` (stitched at 1x).
  - States, made with DOM pokes only (no app setters):
    - `focus-*-state-active-*`: a format `.active`.
    - `export-*-state-exporting-*`: status, progress and Cancel shown the way js/24 shows them.
    - `settings-*-state-open-*`: Visible sections and Your hotkeys open, plus two DOM-built preset rows.
  - Reference pairs: `prep/compare/sweep-1920/<drawer>-pair.png`.
- **Column.** The drawer body's content box is **254 CSS px** at both widths. At 1920 the sidebar is zoomed by `--ui-scale` 1.12 (324 visual px), at 1440 by 1.0. All values here are CSS px before zoom.
- **Text fits** were measured with canvas `measureText` in Michroma at the study's sizes. They are in table T at the end and drive several rows.
- **Hooks.** Every hook named is an existing id, class or structure (checked against `prep/selector-contract.md`). Nothing is renamed. Where script writes inline `style`, the rule needs `!important` (00-shared). The four drawers have no own id. When a rule must be scoped to one drawer, use `.sidebar-section:has(#exportVideoBtn)` (Export), `:has(#photoSafeToggle)` (Display), `:has(#focusModeToggle)` (Focus) or `:has(.ui-vis-block)` (Settings). Most hooks below are unique ids or classes, so `:has()` is rarely needed.
- **Owner "41 (new)"** means a new sheet, `css/41-theme-system-drawers.css` (`@layer theme`, scoped to `html[data-theme="walnut-copper"]`). The button audit already allows button colour there: `js/38-button-audit.js:27` matches `(3[13-9]|4\d)-theme-*.css`. The S rows apply to every drawer. The integrator should give them one home across the g1-g3 specs: this sheet, or the cathode-pane section of `css/31` once W3 has merged.
- **Priority:** P1 is visibly off at a glance, P2 is off on inspection, P3 is polish.
- **Not seen in a web build** (Electron or permission only): Export's Output Folder row; Settings' ComfyUI block, `#openPresetsFolderBtn` and `#userPresetStatus`; Display's `#penWindowMouseReturn`, `#transparentRestartRow` and the pen-screen radio rows. Their rows are specified from source and marked "not seen".
